Lionel Messi: A Short Biography

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He began playing football as a child and joined FC Barcelona’s youth academy, La Masia, in 2000. He made his first-team debut in 2004 and became one of the club’s most celebrated players, winning many domestic and international titles.

Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain in 2021 and moved to Inter Miami CF in 2023. As captain, he led Argentina to victory at the 2022 FIFA World Cup. He also won the Copa América with Argentina in 2021 and 2024. Known for his exceptional dribbling, vision, and goal-scoring, Messi has won multiple Ballon d’Or awards and is widely regarded as one of football’s greatest players.
